# Question 4 — Reflection on AI Use



Write 1–2 paragraphs addressing:

1. Has your perspective on using AI in programming changed over the semester? Briefly explain.
At the start of the semester, I viewed AI coding tools as a potential crutch that might undermine fundamental learning. However, after using them regularly for debugging and code explanation my perspective has shifted dramatically. I now see AI as an empowering pair programmer that accelerates problem solving without replacing critical thinking. For instance, when stuck on a recursive function, AI would walk through the logic step by step, helping me grasp the concept more deeply. This semester taught me that responsible AI use actually reinforces good programming.

2. How do you plan to use AI in future programming or coursework?
Moving forward, I plan to integrate AI as a standard part of my workflow for learning and for  projects. In future coursework, I’ll use AI to quickly prototype different approaches to an assignment, letting me compare readability, efficiency, and memory usage. For personal projects, I intend to leverage AI for tasks like generating unit tests, refactoring messy code, and suggesting architectural patterns. More importantly, I want to use AI as a study buddy: asking it to quiz me on algorithms, simulate code review feedback, or produce small practice problems tailored to weak areas. By treating AI as a collaborator rather than a shortcut.
